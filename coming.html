POPCRAFT · INDEPENDENT ARCHIVE

Good ideas deserve a place to live on.

Popcraft is currently being built as an independent archive dedicated to preserving parts of Splash's history — including its games, tools, music, ideas, and community.

This isn't an official replacement for Splash or Popgun Labs. It's a personal project focused on remembering what was created, the people who were part of it, and the ideas that deserve to be remembered.

Enter the archive About Popcraft
WHAT'S BEING PRESERVED

A little bit of everything that made Splash special.

Popcraft is being built around several parts of the Splash story, with the archive growing over time.

Games

Remembering the games and experiences that helped build Splash's creative community.

Music

Preserving releases, artists, tracks, and the musical side of the Splash story.

Tools

Documenting creative tools such as Splash Pro and other ideas that were part of the ecosystem.

THE COMMUNITY

The people are part of the archive too.

One of Popcraft's goals is to preserve the community history surrounding sPlAsH: Music and Skate on Roblox — beginning with Kai, the creator of the game, and continuing through the players who became part of its story.

Kai

Remembering the creator and where the sPlAsH story began on Roblox.

Stars

Documenting the players, creators, and performers who became part of the community.

Legend+

Preserving Legend, Master, Icon, Immortal, and Ex Celebs achievements when they can be documented.

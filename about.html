Splash is the world's most advanced music technology company. We believe that making music should be simple, intuitive, fun and accessible to everyone.

We've made it our business to find new and inventive ways to inspire the next generation of musicians. This includes 'Splash', our popular game on Roblox, our suite of AI-powered composition and production tools for professional music creators, and creating our own original music, thanks to our first Splash Star, Kai.

OUR ULTIMATE GOAL IS TO HELP YOU TRANSFORM THE FUTURE OF MUSIC.

Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you visit Popcraft and how the independent archive handles information you voluntarily provide.

LAST UPDATED · SEPTEMBER 14, 2026

1. About this Privacy Policy

Popcraft is an independent Splash website archive operated as a personal archival project. This Privacy Policy describes the information practices currently intended for the Popcraft website.

Popcraft does not currently provide user accounts, public user profiles, payment processing, or a Popcraft-hosted social platform.

2. Information you provide

If you contact Popcraft by email, Popcraft will receive the information included in your message, such as your email address, name if provided, and the contents of your message.

This information may be used to respond to your request, investigate an archival correction, address a copyright or rights-related concern, or otherwise communicate with you about the matter you contacted Popcraft about.

3. Technical information

When you visit a website, the hosting, security, or infrastructure providers used to deliver the website may process technical information associated with your connection. This can include information such as an IP address, browser or device information, requested pages, timestamps, and security-related request data.

This information may be processed by the services responsible for hosting, delivering, securing, or maintaining Popcraft. Popcraft does not use this information to create personal profiles of visitors.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

Popcraft does not currently intentionally use advertising cookies or behavioral tracking cookies for its own purposes.

Third-party services used to deliver, secure, or link to content may use their own cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

5. Third-party websites and services

Popcraft contains links to third-party services, including Discord, Roblox, music services, community archives, and other websites.

When you follow an external link, you are leaving Popcraft and interacting with a separate service. Those services have their own privacy practices and policies, which Popcraft does not control.

6. Discord and community services

Popcraft may direct visitors to the Splash community Discord for information about community projects such as the Splash Remake. Popcraft does not operate that Discord server and does not control the information collected or processed by Discord.

If you join or interact with a third-party community service, that service's own privacy policy and terms apply to your activity there.

7. Children and age-related information

Popcraft does not intentionally collect personal information from children through user accounts or registration because the website does not currently provide those features.

Some external services linked from Popcraft may have their own age requirements. Visitors should review and follow the requirements of those services before using them.

8. How information is used

Information voluntarily provided to Popcraft is used only as reasonably necessary to communicate with you, respond to requests, maintain the archive, investigate issues, or address legal and rights-related concerns.

Popcraft does not currently sell personal information or use personal information for targeted advertising.

9. Information sharing

Popcraft does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the website, such as hosting, security, email, or infrastructure providers. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, protect the website, or address security or abuse.

10. Data retention

Information sent directly to Popcraft, such as email correspondence, may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, maintain records, resolve disputes, address rights-related matters, or meet legal obligations.

Technical information processed by hosting or security providers may be retained according to those providers' own policies and operational requirements.

11. Your choices and requests

If you have a privacy-related question or believe Popcraft has received personal information that should be reviewed, you can contact info@showtimecraft.org.

Requests involving information controlled by a third-party service must generally be directed to that service.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Popcraft's website, services, or information practices change. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or concerns regarding Popcraft, contact:

info@showtimecraft.org

PRIVACY FIRST

A simple archive should stay simple.

Popcraft is intended to be an archival website, not a platform built around collecting visitor information. The project aims to keep its own collection of personal information as limited as reasonably possible.

Popcraft is an independent archive preserving parts of Splash's legacy, including its games, tools, music, and community history.

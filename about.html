ABOUT POPCRAFT

A place for keeping good ideas alive.

Popcraft is an independent project dedicated to preserving parts of Splash's history — from its games and music to its creative tools, ideas, and community. It isn't an official replacement for Splash. It's a place to remember what made it special.

THE STORY SO FAR

From Popgun, to Splash, to Popcraft.

2016 — Popgun Labs

An AI-music startup with a big idea

Popgun Labs set out to build AI that could genuinely understand and make music — not just imitate it.

2019 — Splash launches

The idea meets a consumer audience

Splash brought that research to everyday creators through games, creative tools, music experiences, and a growing community of creators and Stars.

2020 — Splash on Roblox

sPlAsH: Music and Skate finds its community

sPlAsH: Music and Skate brought music, skating, performance, and social creativity together on Roblox, creating a community whose history is worth remembering.

2026 — Popcraft

An independent archive begins

Popcraft brings together preserved pieces of Splash's legacy, including its games, tools, music, ideas, and community history. The goal is simple: document what can be remembered and give these projects a place to live on.

WHERE IT STARTED

It starts with the people who made Splash what it was.

From the people who built Splash to the players who filled its worlds, Popcraft's archive is about the people behind the ideas. Kai and the sPlAsH community are an important part of that story, and the Stars archive helps preserve the players who supported the game throughout its years on Roblox.

THE POPCRAFT PLEDGE

Three principles for preserving the legacy.

1. Preserve what can be preserved

Games, music, tools, websites, ideas, and community history deserve to be documented when possible.

2. Be clear about what Popcraft is

Popcraft is an independent archive and project. It does not claim to be Splash, Popgun Labs, or an official replacement for either.

3. Remember the people behind it

The history isn't only about products. Creators, developers, artists, performers, and players are part of the story too.

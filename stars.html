THE STORY BEHIND THE STARS

From Kai to the sPlAsH community.

Before there was a Stars archive, there was a game. Kai created sPlAsH: Music and Skate on Roblox, and the players who joined it became part of the story.

2020 — sPlAsH on Roblox

The community begins

sPlAsH: Music and Skate brought music, skating, performance, and social creativity together on Roblox. Players began building memories and connections inside the game.

The Community

Players became part of the history

As the community grew, players progressed through the game's ranks and became recognizable members of the sPlAsH community.

Legend+

The highest ranks are remembered

Legend, Master, Icon, Immortal, and Ex Celebs represent players who climbed beyond the ordinary progression of the game. This archive records those achievements when they can be documented.

2026 — The Archive

Keeping the memories alive

Popcraft documents the people, music, and history connected to sPlAsH: Music and Skate so that the community's story can continue to be remembered.

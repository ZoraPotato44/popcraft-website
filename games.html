SPLASH REMAKE

A separate project connected to the sPlAsH community.

The Splash Remake is a separate project from Popcraft. Popcraft does not develop, operate, own, or control the remake. If you want the full and most current information about the Splash Remake Demo, check the development announcements provided by the development team in the Splash community Discord.

1. Join the Discord

Join the Splash community Discord to find the latest information about the Splash Remake and its development.

2. Check #dev-announcements

For the full information about the Splash Remake Demo, look in #dev-announcements under the sosoendless category.

3. Follow the team's instructions

Any access, whitelist, testing, age, or other requirements should be followed according to the latest information provided by the remake development team.

PLEASE NOTE

Age and access requirements

You must be 13 or older to use Discord. This requirement is included to help ensure that the Splash community Discord follows Discord's Terms of Service. The Splash Remake Demo may have a separate age requirement. A 16+ requirement has been mentioned as a rough estimate, but it has not been confirmed by the Splash Remake development team. Please check #dev-announcements in the sosoendless category of the Splash community Discord for the latest information.

FROM THE ARCHIVE

A community built around music, skating, and creativity.

SPLASH: Music and Skate became a memorable part of the Roblox community from 2020 through 2026. Popcraft's Games archive exists to document that history and remember the people who helped make the game and its community what they were.

IMPORTANT NOTE

Popcraft is an archive, not the remake.

Popcraft is an independent Splash website archive, combining elements of the old and current Splash website designs while preserving parts of Splash's history, ideas, games, tools, music, and community.

The Splash Remake is a separate development project. Popcraft is not its developer, owner, publisher, or official representative. Information about the remake should be treated as coming from its development team.

For the full and latest information about the Splash Remake Demo, visit #dev-announcements in the sosoendless category of the Splash community Discord.

SPLASH REMAKE DEMO

Want to try the remake?

The remake demo currently uses a whitelist system. To request access, join the Splash community Discord and submit your Roblox username in the #whitelist channel.

1. Join the Discord

Join the Splash community Discord using the button above to find the remake demo information and access instructions.

2. Submit your Roblox username

Go to the #whitelist channel and provide your Roblox username so the remake team can add you to the demo whitelist.

3. Wait for access

Once your Roblox username has been added to the whitelist, follow the remake team's instructions for joining the demo.

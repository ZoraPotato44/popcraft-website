THE COMMUNITY ARCHIVE

The people behind the history matter too.

One part of Popcraft's archive will focus on the people who helped make Splash what it was. That includes Kai, the creator of sPlAsH: Music and Skate, as well as the players and creators who became part of the community.

  • sPlAsH: Music and Skate community history
  • Legend, Master, Icon, Immortal, and Ex Celebs records
  • Music, releases, memories, and community moments
  • The years and milestones that shaped the community
THE POPCRAFT PLEDGE

Good ideas deserve a place to live on.

Popcraft is an independent project and archive. It doesn't claim to be Splash or Popgun Labs, and it isn't an official replacement for either.

Preserve

Document games, music, tools, ideas, and community history whenever possible.

Be honest

Clearly distinguish the independent Popcraft archive from the original organizations and projects.

Remember

Keep the people and communities behind the projects from being forgotten.

Have something for the archive?

If you have something connected to Splash's history that you think belongs here, we'd love to hear from you.

POPCRAFT TOOLS · SPLASH ARCHIVE

Remember the tools that helped people make music.

The Tools archive documents parts of Splash's music-making history, including Splash Pro and the sound packs that were part of the creative experience.

  • Preserving information about Splash Pro and its history
  • Keeping track of Splash sound packs and music resources
  • Linking to existing community archives where appropriate

Sorry, the Splash Pro VST is not available to download as Splash has stopped supporting the Splash Pro VST and web service in 2023 and 2024.

FROM THE ARCHIVE

A record of the tools behind the music.

Splash's creative tools were part of a larger ecosystem of music-making, sound design, and community creativity. Popcraft aims to preserve the history of that ecosystem and point people toward resources that still exist.
